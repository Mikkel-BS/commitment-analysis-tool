# Philosophy editorial audit — 2026-09-20

This is a project editorial review, not independent specialist approval. Validity and premise controversy are separate. Source locators are section/topic locators, not claims that a source endorses our exact encoding.

## R-FUTURE-KNOWLEDGE

Retained only under the temporal, factive reading of foreknowledge and the same domain of future free-action propositions. Present truth is not causal necessity.

## R-OPEN-BIVALENCE

Universal bivalence and its existential denial concern the same propositions and prior assessment time. No general theorem about future semantics is asserted.

## R-TIME-MODELS

Conceptual exclusion under the unqualified same-subject definitions. Hybrid phases require different claims and cannot be represented by this pair.

## R-LIBERTARIAN-DETERMINISM

Downgraded: prior-condition determination and a possibly timeless decree are not automatically the same relation. The temporal/causal bridge remains unresolved.

## R-FATALISM

Retained as a dialectical outline. The general past-fixity sentence does not supply the hard-fact premise or the full necessity-to-inability bridge.

## R-TIMELESS-FOREKNOWLEDGE

Retained as interpretive pressure: a temporal reading conflicts with timelessness, but a relational or loose reading changes the proposition.

## R-IMMUTABLE-TEMPORAL

Retained as interpretive pressure. Temporally ordered states do not alone establish intrinsically different states.

## R-DIVINE-FREEDOM-ALT

Added CREATION-ALTERNATIVE: alternatives in choice must involve omitting or replacing this total created order, not merely different intentions with identical effects.

## R-SIMPLICITY-CREATION

Added necessary existence to the triggering cluster. Identity of willing, necessity of its content, and transfer to effects are still disputed; this remains an objection outline, never a deduction.

## R-MIDDLE-LIBERTARIAN

Removed the actual-action conclusion. Possible libertarian subject matter is an interpretive assumption of the Molinist reading, not a consequence of knowing arbitrary counterfactuals. Downgraded to an explicit interpretive tension when possibility is denied.

## R-HIDDENNESS

A propositional reconstruction based on SEP Hiddenness of God §2, with replies in §§3–4. The complete modal argument is not encoded. Only its propositional inference is mechanically checked. Neither premise is verified. Objections target premises and never prove the opposite conclusion.

## Limits

Direct matching only: no recursive inference, global consistency guarantee, modal theorem proving, or personal belief diagnosis. Incomplete guidance is currently limited to hiddenness. Existing IDs remain stable, but old shared selections are evaluated against the current corpus; links do not pin historical semantics. The middle-knowledge rule now surfaces interpretive pressure rather than an actual-action conclusion.
