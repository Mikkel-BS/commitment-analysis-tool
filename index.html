How to read the analysis

Pressure is not contradiction.

The Atlas keeps formal consequence separate from philosophical argument. It shows a warning only when all of a rule’s explicit premises are selected, and every conclusion retains its provenance.

01

Claims are atomic

Labels such as “Molinist” are only starting points. The analysis operates on the individual propositions you actually select.

02

Strength is visible

Formal and conceptual results are separated from widely defended, contested, and interpretation-dependent arguments.

03

Assumptions stay exposed

A relation fires only when its load-bearing premises are present. Objections and possible replies remain attached to the result.

Editorial principle

The database maps a dialectic; it does not pronounce which position is true. Claims and rules are version-controlled and open to criticism.
